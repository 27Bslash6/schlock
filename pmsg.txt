fix(validator): close two under-blocks the panel found in the rewrite

Both were deny-to-allow against main, and both were this ticket's own defect
class reintroduced by its own fix.

1. A delimiter starting with "-" survived the bare-word check, so "<<'-q'" was
   re-emitted as "<<-q" -- which bash and bashlex both re-lex as the "<<-"
   operator plus delimiter "q". The body then ended at a line reading "q"
   instead of "-q", and every command in between was filed as inert heredoc
   text: a live "curl ... | sh" after the real terminator scored SAFE, against
   BLOCKED on main. The rewrite has to survive RE-LEXING, not just quote
   removal, so the first character is now excluded separately from the rest.

2. ShellCheck is a second consumer of the parsed form and reads delimiters too.
   It cannot parse an interior-quoted delimiter ("<<'E'OF") any more than
   bashlex can -- it answers with SC1044 parse noise instead of findings, so
   the entire ShellCheck tier went silently empty for exactly the commands
   normalisation exists to rescue. On main these reached ShellCheck through the
   fallback's neutered form, so "cat <<'E'OF ... rm -fr /lib" went BLOCKED to
   SAFE. It now gets the normalised command like every other consumer.

Regression guards for both; the two end-to-end ones fail on the pre-fix tree.
The ShellCheck guard deliberately runs without the no-shellcheck fixture.

Not applied, with reasons: passing heredoc_ranges to the multi-segment
no-match fallback (the rangeless scan is the only net for bashlex-vs-bash
divergence -- measured previously as 64 write-then-run shapes and 24
stdin-executing owners going BLOCKED to SAFE), and blanking a non-shell body
before matching (measured at 12x SLOWER, not faster -- the unbounded rule
patterns backtrack over homogeneous whitespace runs).
